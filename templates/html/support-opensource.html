Frequently Asked Questions

How do I format my Excel / CSV file?

Your spreadsheet should have column headers in the first row, such as Email, name, and company. When creating a campaign, you can download our pre-made sample CSV template!

How many emails should I send per day?

For optimal deliverability and to avoid spam filters, we recommend keeping outreach under 300-500 emails per day per email account.

How do automated follow-ups work?

When creating a campaign, click "Add Follow-up" to schedule a second email a few days later. You can set it to send only if the recipient has NOT replied to your first email.
